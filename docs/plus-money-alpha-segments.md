# Plus-money alpha audit, appendix: every segment

Model lab run #102 (`audit:plus-money`), production DB, 2026-11-17. Settled NBA prop picks, each frozen before its game and settled after it. 6,622 main-line picks (80 games, 11 slate days, Nov 6–16); 2,471 alternate-line picks.

**Columns:**
- **Win %:** with a 95% interval from a cluster bootstrap that resamples whole games.
- **ARCHR:** ARCHR's average chance.
- **No-vig:** the books' average de-vigged chance.
- **Break-even:** 1 / decimal odds at the offered price.
- **ARCHR EV:** ARCHR's own expected value at the price (chance × decimal odds − 1).
- **ROI:** flat stakes at the recorded price, with a 95% interval.
- **Calib.:** won minus ARCHR's chance, in points.
- **Won − no-vig:** points won beyond the books' chance (beating the market, adjusted for price).
- **CLV:** the no-vig move toward the pick from capture to close, in points, with the share of closes that moved toward ↑ or away ↓.

**Flags:** ⚠ too few = under 30 picks; ⚠ small = under 200.

The discovery and post periods are both **exploratory** for these questions; see `plus-money-alpha-audit.md`.
