# How the books price, and how we close the gap

This is a working research note, not settled science. Most public writing on
how sportsbooks price is practitioner or vendor content. Where a claim rests on
one blog it is marked **(unverified)**. The roadmap at the end ranks the work by
expected gain per hour.

## 1. Who actually sets the price

- **Market makers and copiers.** A few books originate prices. Pinnacle and
  Circa on game lines take sharp action at low opening limits, then raise limits
  as the number firms up ([Pinnacle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinnacle_Sports)).
  Most retail books (DK, FD, MGM, Caesars) follow moves at the sharp books
  rather than originating game-line prices
  ([thestatsapi](https://thestatsapi.com/odds-api/clv),
  [sharpapi](https://sharpapi.io/learn/line-movement-sports-betting)).
- **Props are different.** Prop lines are mostly generated by algorithm from
  season stats, recent form and the matchup/game environment, with light human
  review. Several books buy props from data vendors, and smaller books copy the
  leaders ([sportswagerblog](https://www.sportswagerblog.com/guides/prop-betting-strategy/),
  [betherosports](https://betherosports.com/blog/prop-betting-strategy)).
  Pinnacle reportedly sources its NFL props from Swish Analytics
  ([Unabated](https://unabated.com/post/the-biggest-mistake-youre-making-when-betting-nfl-player-props),
  **unverified**).
- **Prop limits are low,** so sharp money corrects prop lines more slowly than
  sides and totals. That is why props are where a model can most realistically
  beat the market, and why stale books exist there.

**What this means for us:** on sides and totals, the de-vigged sharp consensus
*is* close to the truth, and our job is mostly to find books lagging it (what
`marketValue.ts` already does). On props, the consensus is noisier and our own
projection can add real information, especially in the first hours after
lines open and right after injury news.

## 2. How a prop line is built

The common recipe, used by books and serious modelers alike:

1. **Minutes / volume first.** Minutes (NBA) or snaps, routes and carries
   (NFL) are the base that every counting stat scales from. Rates multiply,
   pace and the game total set the environment
   ([Stokastic](https://www.stokastic.com/articles/nba-dfs/nba-dfs-research),
   [RotoGrinders](https://rotogrinders.com/lessons/key-inputs-in-an-nba-projections-system-1144825)).
2. **Per-unit rate,** weighted towards recent games and shrunk towards a
   longer baseline. A typical minutes blend weights the last 5 games at about
   35% against season-long minutes
   ([RotoGrinders](https://rotogrinders.com/fantasy/lessons/nba-player-projections)).
3. **Environment from the game lines.** Spread and total drive team plays,
   pass/run split and blowout risk. Books are generally trusted to set the
   totals right, so modelers take them as inputs
   ([RotoGrinders](https://rotogrinders.com/lessons/key-inputs-in-an-nba-projections-system-1144825),
   [4for4](https://www.4for4.com/node/180238)).
4. **Lineup changes.** A starter ruled out redistributes possessions, shots
   and rebounds. The swing on a high-usage teammate can be large
   ([Stokastic](https://www.stokastic.com/articles/nba-dfs/nba-dfs-research)).
5. **A full distribution, not a point.** The line is set near the
   **median**, not the mean. For skewed stats (yards, points for bench
   players, anytime TD) the mean sits above the median, so "projection >
   line" can still be a losing over
   ([Stokastic](https://www.stokastic.com/articles/dfs-strategy/player-props-for-dfs-players),
   [PMC paper on median pricing of game lines](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10306238/)).
   Serious shops simulate thousands of games to get the distribution.
6. **Hold and shading.** Props carry a much bigger hold (often 8–12% two-way)
   than sides. Blind under-betting on passing yards has been the one profitable
   category in a few public samples
   ([PFF](https://www.pff.com/news/nfl-betting-2022-midseason-player-prop-performance-review),
   [4for4](https://www.4for4.com/2023/preseason/key-winning-season-long-player-props)).
   The samples are small, and skew explains part of it (an injury or blowout
   only ever helps the under), so we treat it as a prior to test, not a rule.

## 4. Roadmap (ranked)

Items 1 and 2 shipped on the shared framework (MODEL-FRAMEWORK.md).


1. **Blend the model with the market.** The cheapest big win. Final
   probability = a weighted mix (on the log-odds scale) of our projection and
   the de-vigged consensus, with the weight fitted by backtest per sport and
   stat. Books that copy a stale vendor number get flagged by both signals at
   once. Fit the weight on the closing price, not the result, so a season of
   data is enough.
2. **Game environment from the lines.** NBA: scale minutes for blowout risk
   (spread > 10) and the rate for the game total vs league average. NFL: team
   plays ≈ f(total), pass rate ≈ f(spread) → split into pass attempts and
   rushes, then allocate by each player's share. Coefficients fitted on our own
   history.
3. **Track closing line value.** Snapshot the price when we flag a play and the
   last price before tip. The average CLV of flagged plays is the fastest
   honest grade of the models (it converges in weeks, the win rate takes
   seasons). Show it on `/models` next to the Brier scores.
   Caveat: CLV on books that limit sharps can be contaminated
   ([Action Network](https://www.actionnetwork.com/education/closing-line-value-sports-betting-freedman)).
4. **Rest and schedule.** NBA back-to-backs, 3-in-4s and travel cut minutes for
   veterans. Low cost once the schedule rows exist, and they already do.
5. **Usage redistribution by role, not just "games without".** When samples
   without a teammate are thin, move the absent player's minutes and shots to
   teammates by position and depth chart instead of waiting for 2+ games.
6. **NFL shares.** Add snap %, route participation and target share from box
   scores where ESPN exposes them, so volume projects from role rather than
   last week's totals.
7. **Simulation for correlated markets.** One simulated game feeds every
   player and stat, which unlocks honest same-game-parlay pricing and
   combo props (PRA, rush + rec).

Each step lands with a backtest on the Model lab workflow, and only ships if it
beats the previous model on Brier and log loss out of sample.
