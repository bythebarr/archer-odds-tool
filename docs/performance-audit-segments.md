# Performance audit, appendix: every segment

Model lab run #101 (`audit:performance`), production DB, 2026-11-17. NBA main-line prop picks frozen before each game, settled after it.

Columns, in table order:

- **Bets:** settled hit/miss picks (pushes and voids are set aside).
- **Win %:** the actual win rate, with a game-cluster bootstrap 95% interval.
- **ARCHR avg:** ARCHR's average predicted chance.
- **Actual − ARCHR:** the gap between the two, in percentage points.
- **ROI:** flat stakes at the recorded price, with a 95% interval.
- **Avg odds:** the average price, American.
- **Brier and Log loss:** for ARCHR and for the books' no-vig chance on the same picks.
- **Skill:** books' Brier minus ARCHR's, ×1000 (+ means ARCHR was closer), with a 95% interval.
- **Won − no-vig:** points won over the books' no-vig chance, with a 95% interval.
- **CLV:** the no-vig move toward the pick from capture to close, in points, with the share of closes that moved toward ↑ or away ↓ (the rest did not move).

⚠ too few = under 30 picks; ⚠ small = under 200.

Records, games of history, role and the cautious edge are rebuilt from box scores of earlier games only (see the main report). Everything else was recorded before the game.
